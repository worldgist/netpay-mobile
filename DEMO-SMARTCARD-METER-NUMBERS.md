## Notes

- **Airtime & Data:** Always use `08012345678` (demo user's phone number)
- All smartcard numbers are 10 digits (minimum required)
- All meter numbers are 11 digits (standard Nigerian format)
- These are demo numbers and won't actually receive services
- Use the primary number for the corresponding provider
- You can use any of these numbers for testing - the system will accept them
